The problem

A working integration does not mean your catalog is reconciled.

A sync can finish without errors and still leave hidden differences between your ERP and sales channels. The problem usually appears later — when inventory runs out, a price is wrong or the SKU does not match.

01

Inventory mismatch

A channel shows units your ERP no longer has, or the other way around.

02

Unexpected price

Small differences go unnoticed until they affect margin or conversion.

03

SKU and presence inconsistencies

Missing, duplicated or differently linked items across sources.

FAQ

Questions before buying

No fine print hiding how the product works.

Are my files sent to a server?

Not for the audit. CSV/XLSX files and operational data are processed locally in your browser. Only Pro license status is checked online.

Do I need to provide marketplace or ERP credentials?

No. Sync Guard works with files you choose to import and does not need marketplace credentials.

Does Sync Guard change inventory, prices or listings?

No. It is read-only: it identifies mismatches and shows evidence so you can decide what to fix at the source.
